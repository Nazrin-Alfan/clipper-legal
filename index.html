Clipper Draft Uploader

A private tool for uploading video drafts to the owner's own TikTok accounts through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

How it works: the owner authorizes the app once via TikTok Login Kit. Videos are then uploaded as drafts into the TikTok app's inbox. Every draft is reviewed, captioned, and published manually by the owner inside the TikTok app — nothing is posted automatically.

This is a personal productivity tool, not a public service. There are no other users.
