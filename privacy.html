Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Clipper Draft Uploader is a private tool operated by its owner (Nazrin Al-fansyuri) for his own TikTok accounts.

Data stored

TikTok OAuth tokens (access token and refresh token) are stored on the owner's own server, used solely to upload video drafts via TikTok's official API. No personal data is collected from anyone else.

Data sharing

No data is sold or shared with any third party. No analytics, no tracking.

Data removal

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from TikTok Settings > Security > Manage apps, and delete the stored tokens from the server.
